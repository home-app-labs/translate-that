- The shortcut now finds your text in more cases. If the app in front does not share its selection, Translate That presses ⌘C there and takes the copied text
- Copy first, then press the shortcut: text you copied just before is translated when nothing is selected. Works without the Accessibility permission. Switch it off in Settings if you do not want it
- A notice in the window explains what to do when the Accessibility permission is missing, with a button to allow it
- Text that password managers mark as secret is never taken from the clipboard
- When Google Translate answers "too many requests" (error 429), a second Google address is tried, and the same text is not asked for twice
- The Accessibility notice goes away by itself once the permission is given
- Settings scroll, so everything fits

**Install:** download `Translate-That.pkg` and open it. macOS blocks it the first time because the app is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". Pictures of each step are on https://homeapplabs.com/install/

Works on macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
